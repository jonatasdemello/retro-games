4/3 = 1.333
16/9 = 1.7777

4:3 = 1.33 (The width is 33% longer than the height)
16:9 = 1.77 (The width is 77% longer than the height)

4:3 (Standard): For every 4 units of width, there are 3 units of height. It looks like an old CRT television screen.
16:9 (Widescreen): For every 16 units of width, there are 9 units of height. It looks like a modern TV, computer monitor, or movie screen.

Common Uses
4:3: Retro video games, older TV shows (pre-2000s), iPad screens, and default photography modes on most smartphones.
16:9: High-definition (HD) TV broadcasting, modern movies, YouTube videos, and widescreen computer monitors.
