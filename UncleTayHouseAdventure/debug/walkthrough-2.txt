*** UNCLE TAY'S HOUSE ADVENTURE ***
***********************************

Find treasures and valuables in your mad uncle tays' house
Type simple commands to move around:
   NORTH, SOUTH, EAST, WEST, UP, DOWN
   or just: S, E, W, U, D.
Type two word commands (verb + action) to interact with objects:
TAKE BOOK, DROP BOOK, INVENTORY, LOOK, READ, MOVE
Some commands are complex:
 'MOVE THE HUBCAP WITH THE SPANNER'

Possible commands:
    NORTH, SOUTH, EAST, WEST, UP, DOWN, N, S, E, W, U, D,
    I, INVENTORY, SCORE, JUMP, HELP, EXIT31
    TAKE, DROP, LOOK, READ, EXAMINE, UNLOCK, EAT, SPIN,
    MOVE, OPEN, TIE, OIL, PUT, LEFT, CENTER, RIGHT
